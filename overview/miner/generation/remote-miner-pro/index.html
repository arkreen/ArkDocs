What is Remote Miner Pro​

The Remote Miner Pro (RMP) functions similarly to the Remote Miner (RM), as both are fractional miners operating remotely. However, unlike RM and Lite Remote Miner (LRM), RMPs are exclusively powered by solar photovoltaic plants registered with REC standard organizations (such as I-REC Registries) that partner with Arkreen, resulting in more stable and efficient power generation.

Each RMP has a capacity of 500 watts (0.5 kW) and a 1-year subscription lifespan starting from the purchase date.

RMPs also feature a dedicated reward pool that expands as more RMPs are onboarded, up to a predefined limit. When the number of RMPs is below this limit, each RMP earns a fixed quantity of AKRE rewards per day, regardless of the amount of green energy it generates. When the reward pool expands to the limit, every RMP equally shares the capped rewards afterwards. Additionally, rewards for RMPs are distributed on a daily basis rather than hourly.

Like RM and LRM, RMP can issue AREC/ART against the green energy amount it generates.

Reward Rules​

RMP rewards are distributed once per day. All RMPs (except expired ones) will qualify for the rewards. Until the RMP quantity reaches the reward pool expansion limit (1000 RMPs in current trial stage), each RMP earns a fixed amount of AKRE rewards per day (so far, 300 AKRE per RMP). The exact reward amount will be adjusted regularly. For instance, at the beginning of the trial, if a user has 3 RMPs, the user will receive 300 x 3 = 900 AKRE per day.
