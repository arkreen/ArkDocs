Arkreen Newcomer Guide

Welcome to Arkreen ecosystem! We will quickly guide you through your first time on the dashboard.​

Step1: Get Your First Arkreen Miner​

1.1 Onboarding Welcome Gift- Claim your first Lite Remote Miner​

After logging in to https://console.arkreen.com/, the first thing you need to do is complete the first task in the Mission popup by clicking the "Claim" button on the right side of "Onboarding Welcome Gift". We have prepared a three-month Lite Remote Miner for every new member joining the Arkreen community. This will help you quickly understand Arkreen Miner products.

Please Click "Claim" and "Tweet" to share with your community.

Please note that the Remote Miner and the Lite Remote Miner are different. The Remote Miner is our commercial product.

Remote Miner:

  • Remote Miners are the primary mining units that can be purchased on the Arkreen platform.
  • They contribute to the generation of Green Energy and the mining of AKRE tokens.
  • Remote Miners operate automatically once purchased and do not require additional activation.

Lite Remote Miner:

  • Lite Remote Miners are a variant that is only available through Airdrops, Campaigns, Missions and other special actions announced on Arkreen's official channels.
  • They are a more lightweight version compared to Remote Miners, with limited rated capacity and lifespan.

1.2 Your First Digital Miner​

Click Arkreen's Shop by navigating to https://console.arkreen.com/#/shop and choose from various cool products. For the best entry, we recommend a product from the lowest price range, like the remote miner or a CSP-remote miner if one is on sale.

Select 'Buy Now' and choose your preferred payment method (USDC.e, USDT, POL). Confirm the transaction to acquire your Remote Miner.

Refer to the provided video guide for visual guidance: https://youtu.be/EZwctMcdXXI?feature=shared

1.4 Get Your Referral Code​

Navigate in the Console menu to the referral section or use the direct link: https://console.arkreen.com/#/referral

You can use this personal link to spread the message and get bonuses if people use it and buy products. Promote it on social media, communities and to friends now and build your exclusive Arkreen network!

For each remote miner being purchased by your friend through your referral link, you can claim a Lite Remote Miner as a gift. Once claimed, you can withdraw your Lite Remote Miners gift at https://console.arkreen.com/#/gift. See Referral Lite Remote Miners Free Gift for details.

Step 2: Check Rewards on Dashboard​

Navigate to Arkreen's dashboard at https://console.arkreen.com/#/dashboard to monitor your miners. Once purchased, they will operate automatically without activation. Be aware that it can take 24-48 hours for each digital product to catch up in onboarding and show all metrics. The dashboard displays statistics such as your Green Energy production and your environmental impact. Further details about your miners, including onboarding time, address, and AKRE balance, can be found by scrolling down.

Statistics for Your Miners

  • Peak Power: The sum of the peak power of every miner in Energy Generation Category, where the peak power of a miner is the actual highest green generation power it has ever reached since onboarding.
  • Green Energy: The amount of green energy produced from solar panels.
  • Impact: The amount of carbon emissions that are avoided thanks to your green energy.
  • Miners: The total quantity of all your miners of all categories.
  • RE Generators: The quantity of your miners of Energy Generation Category.
  • Plugs: The quantity of your Smart Plugs of Energy Consumption Category.

Filtering the dashboard On the right side of your dashboard overview, you can filter all your miners based on their type or their status.

Step 3: AKRE Token​

3.1 Wallet and Contract​

The AKRE token has the contract address 0xE9c21De62C5C5d0cEAcCe2762bF655AfDcEB7ab3 on Polygon. While different wallets can be used in your own choice, we recommend using MetaMask and navigating it on the Polygon network.

3.2 Withdraw AKRE​

Visit Arkreen's withdrawal page at https://console.arkreen.com/#/withdraw , click on the Withdraw button, sign the request, and confirm the transaction to receive your AKRE tokens. If withdrawal fails, follow How to handle with failed withdrawal to retry.

3.3 Staking​

Navigate to the Simple Staking section in the Arkreen console at: https://console.arkreen.com/#/stake You will find different Staking pools in the horizontal top navigation where you can choose from. Once you found the most suitable, Click on the "Stake" button and pick the allocation in the popup for the amount you want to stake and confirm it at bottom. Sign the requested transactions and your stake is locked in. You can withdraw your rewards at any time via this menu, but you can only unstake once the period is finished. You will see a countdown on the right side until the unstake becomes available. If you don't unstake after the period is finished, and a new round of the same staking pool later starts, your stake in that pool will be automatically staked in the new round.

Step 4: Get Your ART​

4.1 Minting ART with Green Energy Generated by Arkreen Miner​

To be able to mint ART you need devices that generate energy in form of kWh. Most important examples here are some of the Arkreen digital producty like the Remote Miners or a CSP Remote Miners. Head to the AREC website at https://arec.arkreen.com/#/quick and follow the Quick Start Guide. Approve AKRE for minting transaction fees, issue generated green energy to mint AREC NFT, and liquidize AREC NFT to receive ERC20 fungible token ART. Wait for the confirmation of your ART status certified by the Arkreen network.

Refer to the provided video guide for visual assistance: https://youtu.be/-9-EFtG_jiw?feature=shared

4.2 ART Issuance Count and Rank​

Navigate to the Rank section in the Arkreen console at: https://console.arkreen.com/#/rank

Here you see your issuance data and ranking in the ecosystem. Watch out for any incentive programs that might qualify for bonuses.

4.3 Using ART​

ART is used for different things in the ecosystem. Important examples here are greening in GreenBTC.Club but also the offset on the PGP Smart Plug Miners. ART is a very important asset for certain functions

Step 5: GreenBTC.Club​

5.1 Greening Bitcoin Blocks​

In the GreenBTC.Club App website, select a Zone to green in and click 'Green Now'..

Choose your payment method (USDC.e, USDT, ART), confirm the transaction, and connect your wallet on the GreenBTC.Club App website. You will then receive a mystery box animation after successfully greening in the zone. This box will be opened automatically during the animation and give you a point score win. All points accumulated during the day will count towards your Play To Impact rating and give you prizes on the next day! Head to the guidebook About GreenBTC.Club V2.

Refer to the provided video guide for visual assistance: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bIPpMV-Je9o

5.2 Play to Impact​

Your point scores qualify you for a share of the total prize pool. Daily settlements will be made, and rewards will be automatically transferred to your greenbtc account on the next day UTC 0:00-1:00.

Explore further details about GreenBTC's long-term incentive program by clicking on the following link: https://docs.greenbtc.club/v2-getting-started/play-to-impact

Check the latest news on Arkreen's Discord and X (formerly Twitter) account to see if any special campaigns have added short term incentives.

Step 6: Special Telegram Apps​

In the Arkreen adjacent Green BTC ecosystem there are also Telegram bot based apps like the "PePe Airdrop" or the "MEME Gift" which serve different engagement aspects. With the PePe Airdrop you can collect points, level up, do free greening and many more exciting things. You can also earn PePe that you can withdraw on the GreenBTC.Club website with gems earned from various tasks or from the daily PePe greening pool.

Best to try it out yourself at: https://t.me/pepe_coin_mining_bot/

Engage in Arkreen events by sharing your contributions on social media. Users who post their accomplishments stand a chance to receive rewards during special Arkreen events. This step encourages community involvement and recognition of participants' efforts.
