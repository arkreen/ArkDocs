Community Solar Program

Objective​

To harness the power of the Web3 community and bring the vision of building DePIN (Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks) together, Arkreen Network has introduced the Community Solar Program, introducing a novel green energy generation node: the Plant Miner. This initiative encourages individuals worldwide who are concerned about climate change to integrate their own solar photovoltaic (PV) power systems into the Arkreen network. Doing so, we can build global renewable energy resources in a DePIN way and develop ReFi applications for public goods.

CSP Application Process​

Step 1: Buy 20 Remote Miners as CSP Purchase Vouchers and get immediate Rewards!​

ORDER PLANT MINER

Getting Started with CSP: Your Journey Starts Now

Buy 20 remote miners as CSP vouchers to activate your CSP license (Order Link). Once your Plant Miner is active, the remote miners will be deactivated; refer to the CSP Payment Safeguard Plan document for details on API integration and smart meters.

Step2: Choose Your Plant Miner Integration Mode​

We currently support two integration modes for Plant Miner:

  • Smart Meter hardware mode, suitable for any inverter with AC output power below 40kW.
  • API Acquisition mode, suitable for inverter brands:
    • Enphase
    • Huawei
    • SolarEdge
    • SolaX Power

The table below compares the Smart Meter and API Acquisition modes:

ModePrice
Smart Meter$199

Arkreen covers all delivery expenses; however, in some regions, users may need to manage customs clearance procedures.
API Acquisition$50/per year subscription

From the second year onwards, CSP API Acquisition subscribers have the option to determine whether to renew their subscription. The renewal fee will only cover the API subscription fee, currently $50. Any subsequent price updates will be promptly communicated to you.

Step 3: Fill Out the Arkreen CSP Form to Share Your Details​

Please complete the form below to submit your application:

ARKREEN COMMUNITY SOLAR PROGRAM APPLICATION FORM

To get your plant miner up and running quickly, make sure to fill out the following info carefully:

  • Contact information, such as Email address and Telegram account.
  • Location of the power plant: country/region and city.
  • Photos of solar panels, inverter interface and nameplate.
  • Extra info, like grid connection status and any third-party monitoring services.

Important:

Individuals sharing personal solar photovoltaic photos must ensure they are authentic and comply with local laws. Images must accurately depict personal assets and adhere to regulations to maintain integrity in the Web3 community.

CSP Onboarding Process​

Once you've submitted the CSP form, the Arkreen support team will get in touch via Telegram or Discord to help you with everything you need. This includes hardware delivery, API setup, and marketing plans.

CSP Rewards​

Plant Miner Rewards​

Plant Miner rewards are directly tied to the rewards earned by the Remote Miners it supports. Click the link to learn how to increase your Plant Miner’s rewards.

Once the staking requirement is fulfilled, Remote Miners based on your solar PV system will be available for sale in 3 batches: 20%, 30%, 30%. For example, for a 5kW solar PV system, the Remote Miners in the 3 batches consist of 20 Remote Miners, 30 Remote Miners, and 30 Remote Miners. The remaining 20% capacity will be reserved as the target for additional Remote Miners.

CSP Staking Trial​

CSP Staking trial started since mid June, 2024. We would like to express our heartfelt thanks for your continued support as we successfully completed the current trial of the CSP staking service. Based on the feedback from the community, we are planning the improvement and upgrade for CSP staking service.

However, these changes will take some time to development and implement. To minimize any potential impact on the community, the CSP Staking services (except for unstaking and withdrawal) will be taken offline at 08:00 UTC on August 17, 2024. Once the services are offline, all currently active stakes will be unlocked, and no new rewards will be issued. We encourage you to unstake your tokens on time.

We will sharing the time plan and detail of next trial of CSP Staking services with you in the near future.

It is important to note that the discontinuation of the CSP Staking services will not affect Arkreen Community Solar Program itself. The operation and rewards of all CSP stations that are already online, as well as the Remote Miners issued based on these CSP stations, will remain unaffected.

If you have any inquiries, don’t hesitate to reach out to our team members in either our Telegram (https://t.me/Arkreen_Network) or Discord community (https://discord.gg/5cXtB9UB9f).
